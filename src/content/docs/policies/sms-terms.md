## Program description

By opting in, you agree to receive text messages from Hikma Health related to your use of our services. These may include appointment notifications, two-factor authentication (2FA) codes and secure sign-in links, patient referral notifications, and updates to your account or schedule.

## How to opt in

You may opt in to receive SMS messages in person at the clinic where you work or are visiting, or by completing our SMS consent form (online or its paper equivalent). By providing your mobile number and consent, you confirm you are the account holder for that number or have authorization to provide it.

## Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on activity. During busy periods you may receive up to 20 messages per day; during quiet periods you may receive no messages for a day or more.

## Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Such charges are billed by and payable to your mobile carrier. Please contact your carrier for details about your plan. Hikma Health is not responsible for any charges incurred.

## Opting out

You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying **STOP** to any message. After you send STOP, we will send a confirmation message and then stop sending SMS messages to you. You may opt back in at any time by enrolling again.

## Help

For help or more information, reply **HELP** to any message or contact us at [ally@hikmahealth.org](mailto:ally@hikmahealth.org).

## Carriers and delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to effective transmission by your mobile carrier and is not guaranteed.

## Privacy

Your information is handled in accordance with our [SMS Privacy Policy](/docs/policies/sms-privacy-policy). We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or opt-in information with third parties for marketing purposes.
